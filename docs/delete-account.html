Delete your Revive account

Revive Fitness App  ·  Account and data deletion

Delete from the app

  1. Open Revive and sign in.
  2. Tap your avatar on the Home screen to open Profile.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out.

Delete without the app

Email privacy@revivefit.app from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Revive account". We will confirm and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account and profile (name, email, date of birth, gender, height, goals)
  • Food logs, workout logs and exercise sets, body stats
  • Mood logs, sleep logs, and wearable connections
  • Friend connections and subscription records

Data is removed from our live database immediately. Copies in encrypted backups expire automatically within 30 days. AI coach chat history is stored only on your device and is removed when you sign out or uninstall the app.

What is kept

Payment and transaction records held by Google Play or Stripe are kept by those providers as required by law. Deleting your Revive account does not cancel a Google Play subscription — cancel it in the Play Store under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
